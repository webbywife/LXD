Questions

Before you sign up.

Why not just use ChatGPT directly?

You can — but you're on your own for curriculum alignment, and you'll get plain text you still have to reformat into a usable lesson plan. SKOOLED‑AI maps directly to official DepEd/MATATAG competencies (or Common Core, UK, ACARA, IB), and outputs a structured lesson plan plus SCORM, GIFT, QTI, or PPTX files you can actually upload to an LMS or present from — not a chat transcript.

How accurate is the DepEd/MATATAG alignment?

The competency, content standard, and performance standard data comes from the official MATATAG curriculum guides — 3,567 competencies across 19 subjects. The AI generates the lesson content itself, but the standards it's aligning to are the real ones, not inferred from general training data.

Is my data private and secure?

Your account, lesson content, and generated files are tied to your login and aren't shared publicly unless you explicitly share a syllabus link. We don't sell user data.

Do I need to pay to use it?

No — every account starts on a free tier with 5 generations per month, full access to every curriculum standard and export format. A paid Pro tier for higher volume is coming; free access isn't going away.

Can I use this outside the Philippines?

Yes. MATATAG/DepEd alignment is the deepest feature, but Common Core, UK National Curriculum, ACARA, and IB are all supported as full curriculum standards, not an afterthought.
